About Me

Hi there! I'm Nikki and it's a pleasure to share my portfolio with you. As for a little background about me, I'm currently a student at BCIT while also freelancing as a junior UI/UX designer. I previously attended University of British Columbia where I pursued a degree in Ancient Roman, Greek and Near Eastern Studies. My passion for UI/UX is fuelled by my desire to see digital spaces become accessible for everyone.

Design Process

My designs maintain a balance between sophisticated UI while prioritising accessible UX. I work with clients on both web and mobile applications. Not only do I help my client with designing their dream website or app, but I'm also excited to announce that I'll be expanding my services to include online marketing services(such as logo design and SEO). Both my extensive education at BCIT in UI/UX research and my freelance work has prepared me for any challenge thrown my way.

Technical Skills

I am proficient in a vast number of tool suites that are essential to web and mobile development such as Figma and Adobe Illustrator. Below I discuss how each of these tools are integrated within my work flow.

Figma

Within Figma, I collaborate with clients to design low-fi wireframes where we can hammer out the beginning stages of their vision before proceeding to develop a hi-fi wireframe where we then start finalizing the site layout.

Adobe Illustrator

Within Illustrator, I aid clients with the branding side of things. I typically start with a rough sketch of potential logos and other visual elements after brainstorming with the client. After coming up with a number of design variations,I will confer with the client and once we have settled on a favourite, I will then begin a final version in Illustrator. Sometimes it takes more a dozen final drafts before we finally get it right. But it's always a satisifying process to see through from start to finish.
